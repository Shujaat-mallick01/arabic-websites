## Delivery within Bahrain
Delivery takes one working day to all governorates, for BHD 1.500, and is free for orders over BHD 25.000.

## Shipping to GCC countries
| Country | Fee | Free over | Typical time |
|---|---|---|---|
| Saudi Arabia | BHD 3.500 | BHD 40.000 | 2–5 working days |
| Kuwait | BHD 4.000 | BHD 45.000 | 2–3 working days |
| UAE | BHD 4.000 | BHD 45.000 | 2–4 working days |
| Qatar | BHD 4.500 | BHD 45.000 | 2–4 working days |
| Oman | BHD 4.500 | BHD 45.000 | 3–5 working days |

## Dispatch times
Orders confirmed before 1 pm Bahrain time ship the same day, Saturday to Thursday. Later orders ship on the next dispatch day. Hemming adds two working days.

## Customs duties
Import duties or VAT may apply under the destination country's rules and are paid by the recipient.

## Pickup from the studio
Orders can be collected free of charge from the Seef studio. They are ready four hours after confirmation and held for seven days.
