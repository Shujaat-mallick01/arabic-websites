In wedding custom, the jalwa is the bride's unveiling; the word comes from polishing a mirror until it shines. We built a calm lounge in Al Shati around that idea: warm light, arched mirrors and stylists who know what suits you.

## Why Jalwa
- Every service shows its time and price before you book.
- Choose your stylist and room, or let us assign the first free one.
- Open every day until 2 am.
- A women's entrance and valet parking.
