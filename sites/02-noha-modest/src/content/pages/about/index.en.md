Dareesha is the Gulf word for a window. We started on Instagram, where our stories were the "window" onto our pieces. Today we have a site with a real cart and checkout, but the idea is the same: clear pieces at clear prices, from our window to your door.

We photograph every shayla and scarf in natural window light, so the colour you see is the colour that arrives. Every piece lists its fabric, care and measurements in centimetres.

## Where we are
A small studio in Seef, Manama, with an order pickup point open Saturday to Thursday.

## How we work
- Orders placed before 1 pm ship the same day.
- We choose every fabric ourselves: satin, gauze, cotton and washed linen.
- Every order is wrapped in tissue with a card showing its arrival date.
