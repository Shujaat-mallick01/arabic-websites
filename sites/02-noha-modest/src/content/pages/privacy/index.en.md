title: Privacy policy
description: How we collect, use and protect your data.
---

We protect your personal data in line with Bahrain's Personal Data Protection Law.

## What we collect
Your name, phone number, delivery address and, if you give it, your email, used only to fulfil and communicate about your order.

## How we use it
To prepare, ship and update you on your order. We never sell or share your data, except with couriers and payment gateways as far as needed to fulfil the order.

## Payments
Card details are processed directly by the payment gateway. We never see or store them.

## Your rights
You may ask to see, correct or delete your data by contacting us on WhatsApp or by email.
