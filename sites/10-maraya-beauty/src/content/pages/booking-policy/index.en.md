title: Booking and cancellation policy
description: Deposits, cancellations and lateness.
---

## Deposit
The deposit is part of the service price and is deducted on arrival. It is thirty percent of the price, at least SAR 50, rounded up to the nearest riyal. Bridal packages have a fixed deposit.

## Changes and cancellations
Appointments may be changed or cancelled free of charge at least twenty-four hours ahead; the deposit then becomes credit valid for sixty days. Deposits are not refunded for cancellations within twenty-four hours or for no-shows.

## Lateness
Arriving more than fifteen minutes late may shorten or reschedule the service.

## Bridal packages
Bridal deposits are non-refundable. The date may be changed once free of charge if the change is made thirty or more days ahead and the suite is available.

## Prices
All prices are in Saudi riyals and include 15% VAT.
