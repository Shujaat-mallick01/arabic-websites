---
title: Exchange and returns policy
description: Exchange and return conditions, time limits and refunds.
---

Customers may request an exchange or return within fourteen days of delivery, provided the piece is unworn and carries its original tags.

## Not returnable
- Shaylas and scarves once unwrapped, for hygiene reasons.
- Shaylas and underscarves, for hygiene reasons.
- Sale items, which can be exchanged only.

## How to return
- Within Bahrain: free exchange at the studio, or courier collection for BHD 1.500.
- GCC: the customer ships the piece back, or buys a prepaid label for BHD 4.000.

## Refunds
Refunds go to the original payment method within seven working days of inspection. Cash-on-delivery orders are refunded by bank transfer. Original shipping fees are not refunded.
